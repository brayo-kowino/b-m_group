What We Do

Save Together, Grow Together

B&M Group is a private savings and lending platform. We follow strict rules, keep careful records, and use a secure web portal to help members save money and borrow when needed.

When you join, your savings combine with other members to create a fund. You can borrow from this fund, and you also can earn interest based on your performance during the year.

Safe and Secure

Your money records are protected with strong security inside the system.

Watch Your Money Grow

See your savings and earnings increase in real time on your dashboard.

Governance

Basic & Group Policies

View Full Constitution

Monthly Savings

All members must save money each month, this helps build a strong credit score and increases your chances of getting approved for loans.

Borrowing Rules

You can borrow up to twice the amount you have saved. You must meet certain criteria to qualify for a loan.

Missing Payments

If you don't pay your loan on time, your savings will be used to cover the missed payments, and extra charges will keep growing on accounts that are late.
