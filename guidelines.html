1. Why we use clear rules

B&M Group uses simple payment rules to avoid arguments. Late payments can cause unfair treatment and stress.

The portal records payments and checks them. It does not accept excuses. This keeps the group fair and protects the money. Discipline before access.

2. Payment rules

Each member must pay KSH 70 per week, plus KSH 10 for each extra day in the month. You must pay and report the payment.

  1. Send money to the official B&M Bank/M-Pesa account.
  2. Open the Member Portal right away.
  3. Type the exact amount and the M-Pesa transaction code.
  4. Send it to Admin for checking.
The system check: The portal checks your account on the 7th and 21st of each month. If you are behind, it sends a warning.

3. Backup reporting

If you paid but cannot use the portal, use offline reporting. This keeps you from getting penalties.

Offline SMS/WhatsApp format

Send this exact message to an Executive Admin (Brian or Maximilla):

B&M PAYMENT REPORT
Name: [Your Name]
Amount: [KSH Amount]
M-Pesa Code: [e.g., SGH1234567]

* The Admin will check the code and add the payment to your account. This stops the warning.

4. Loans and leaving the group

  • Borrowing limit: Members can borrow up to twice their savings if they have at least KSH 500.
  • Interest: All loans have a flat 15% fee.
  • Leaving: To leave the group, submit an application through the portal. No exits if you have an unpaid loan.
